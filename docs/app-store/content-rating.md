# Content rating notes

The app is a reference map of public agency data. Expected result: **4+**
(Apple) and **Everyone / PEGI 3 / USK 0** (Google Play, IARC questionnaire).

## Apple age rating questionnaire

Answer **None** to every content item: cartoon or realistic violence,
sexual content, profanity, horror or fear themes, alcohol, tobacco or drugs,
gambling, contests, medical information, mature themes.

- **Unrestricted web access:** No. Links to agency websites open in the
  system browser, not inside the app.
- **User-generated content / messaging:** No.
- **Made for Kids:** No (general audience).

Note for reviewers if asked: layers describe where wildlife agencies record
wolves and other predators. There are no images or descriptions of animals
being hurt, and no hunting instructions; management areas are shown as the
agencies publish them.

## Google Play: IARC questionnaire

- **Category:** Reference, News, or Educational.
- Violence, fear, sexuality, language, controlled substances, gambling: **No**.
- **Users can interact or exchange content:** No.
- **Shares the user's current location with other users:** No.
- **Allows purchases of digital goods:** No.
- **Unrestricted internet access (web browser or search engine):** No.

## Google Play: other App content forms

- **Ads:** No ads.
- **Target audience:** 18+ (or 13+ if you prefer a wider audience; there is no
  content that needs a higher rating, but choosing under 13 brings the
  Families policy into scope).
- **News app:** No.
- **Government app:** No. The data comes from government agencies, but the
  app is published by BitPrairie, not an agency. Say so in the description
  if asked.
- **Health / financial features:** None.
